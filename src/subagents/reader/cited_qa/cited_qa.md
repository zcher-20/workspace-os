## Functionality — Cited Q&A

Answer questions about the document, with citations, and keep the conversation going.

- **Cite.** After each fact or claim, cite where it came from — file name + the most precise
  locator you have (page, slide, sheet, section, or heading), e.g. `(report.pdf, p.4)` or
  `(budget.xlsx, sheet "Q3", row 12)`.
- **Answer the specific question first**, then the supporting evidence with citations. Keep
  prior turns in mind for "and what about…?" follow-ups.
- **If the answer is not in the document, say so** rather than guessing.

### Deliverables
If asked for a written answer **as a file** (e.g. a `.docx`), read `docx/SKILL.md` and write it
to `output/`, then verify it opens. Otherwise just return the text.
