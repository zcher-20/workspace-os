## Functionality — Summarization

Produce a summary of the document, grounded and cited.

- Lead with a **3–6 sentence executive summary**, then structured highlights (bullets/sections).
- Carry **citations for the key claims** — file name + the most precise locator you have
  (page, slide, sheet, section, or heading).
- Match the length and focus the user asked for; don't pad. Prefer the document's own figures
  and wording over paraphrase when precision matters.
