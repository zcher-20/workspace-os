## Functionality — Conflict detection

Compare the extracted facts across sources and surface every disagreement.

1. **Compare across sources.** Line up facts about the same entity/metric/topic from different
   documents. Identify where they **agree**, **partially differ**, or **directly contradict**.
2. **Flag every conflict explicitly.** For each contradiction state: the claim in question,
   **each** source's version with its citation, and the nature of the conflict (different
   number, date, conclusion, definition, scope…). Do not silently pick one.
3. **Assess reliability when sources disagree.** Weigh by recency, authority/source type
   (audited vs draft, official vs forwarded), and specificity. Explain *why* you lean one way;
   if it can't be resolved from the documents alone, say it needs human judgment.
